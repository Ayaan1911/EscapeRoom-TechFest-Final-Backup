# Targeted changes: Trail event feed, Top 12 projector, Forensic Snapshot (and the one-group Evidence Wall)

Scope of this pass: four changes only. Not touched: Round 1 scoring, Round 2 (scoring, 60-minute format, Injects 1-4, Kill Switch), Top 10 qualification, jury marking, the Containment Key, credentials, the GHOST//OS design.

## 1. Trail: readable event feed under the timeline
- **Kept:** the timeline (activity overview bars, per-type lanes, markers with shapes, time-slice zoom, session lines), the filters, the Event console and Event inspector windows, the evidence pinning, all scoring.
- **Added:** "What happened" feed directly under the timeline (`src/components/game/trail-feed.tsx`, in page flow; on wide screens it sits in the left column with the task column beside it). Each row: time (UTC), event type, outcome badge (icon + text: Allowed / Denied / Challenged), account, source IP, a one-line plain-language description, event ID and session ID, and an Inspect button. Rows are grouped under hour headings; 30 are listed at first, "Show 30 more" / "Show all 144" reveal the rest (all 144 events stay available; they render in well under a second).
- **Synchronized:** the feed shows exactly the filtered events, narrowed to the timeline's zoomed slice (the timeline reports its window; "Show the whole night" restores everything). Clicking a marker selects and scrolls to its row (growing the list if needed) and opens the inspector as before; clicking a row highlights its marker. Rows are keyboard operable (Tab, Enter).
- **No new clues:** the description is a FIXED sentence per event type (the same for every event of that type, every team), e.g. "A session token was issued." The free-text `details`, `method`, `device` and `conditionalAccess` fields are NOT listed because they carry the Tamper Alert text and policy wording; they remain in the inspector. A legend sentence states that Allowed/Denied/Challenged says nothing about intent. No red/green correctness styling beyond the existing outcome badges.
- **One scroll:** no new inner scrollbars.

## 2. Evidence Wall: one assigned group per team
See `ghost404-evidence-wall.md`. 30 teams → 10/10/10, one private fragment, one matching public card, additive migration `20261127000000_insider_group_assignment`. No team accounts or passwords were created; the final roster is prepared later with one organizer action.

## 3. Projector: Top 12 as the main content
- **Layout** (`src/components/admin/broadcast.tsx`, `src/lib/game/projector-board.ts`, CSS appended to `command.css`): compact top band (brand, phase label, small server-driven timer with a thin progress line in Round 2), then the board as the main region: two columns of six, large ranks and names, tabular scores, restrained brass/green emphasis for the top three, shared ranks shown with "=", a footer with the "+N more teams" count and a note when teams tied at the cut are not listed. Fits 1920x1080 and 1366x768 without scrolling. Rows glide to new positions (no jumping; none with reduced motion). A stale connection announces itself and dims the board.
- **What is shown, per scene** (`selectBoard`, unit-tested): Round 1 live: "LIVE / PROVISIONAL - ROUND 1" only if the organizer switched "Round 1 standings" on; Round 1 closed: same, "PROVISIONAL / under review"; after qualification and during Round 2: the confirmed finalists (never padded to twelve, no scores) if "finalist names" is on, else the Round 1 standings labelled "ROUND 2 NOT PUBLISHED" if standings are on; results: the published Round 2 results only. No ranking at all in standby; nothing leaks while a switch is off (a calm status screen is shown instead). **Pens down** stays a full-screen moment.
- **Unchanged rules:** server-authoritative clock (never starts on page load), the audited default-OFF publication switches, no invented tie-breakers, no live Round 2 standings from unmarked paper scores, no automatic Round 1 + Round 2 total.

## 4. Chapter 1: Forensic Snapshot replaces the QR card
- **Before:** the Inbox panel said "QR forensic snapshot / Printed card -> capture ID" and linked to `/clue/inbox` in a new tab (players) or to the organizer-only `/admin/inbox-kit` (Demo Mode).
- **Now:** panel "Forensic Snapshot - Open the archived forensic capture and inspect the evidence." with a button "Open Forensic Snapshot" opening in the same tab. Players: `/clue/inbox` (team session required, evidence chosen from the team's own variant, a query string cannot select another variant). Demo Mode: `/admin/demo/round1/inbox/snapshot?v=` (organizer only, sample variant, no team data). Both render the same `SnapshotView`: the original capture manifest (evidence ID such as CAP-A-17, URL, title, campaign, time, redirect, recipient, inert form action) and the inert page reconstruction, in GHOST//OS styling, with "Return to Inbox".
- **Preserved:** the evidence content, the capture ID the player must still read and enter (`inbox.entry.snapshotId`), scoring and progression. Clicking the button completes nothing.
- **Removed:** every "printed QR / card kit" wording in the player flow and in the hint text. `/admin/inbox-kit` (organizer-only, optional QR generator) is left in place and relabelled "no longer needed". No phone, scanner, external QR service or Internet is involved.
